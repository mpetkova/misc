1. Describe a systems or infrastructure decision you initially thought was right but later changed your mind on. Why?
I developed a multi-step AWS workflow to run inference on a weather dataset. The pre- and post-processing steps worked well, however we had capacity issues while running the ML inference steps.
these were run in parallel on AWS Batch and I opted to use spot instances for this step to reduce costs. The step however required 80 iterations and we were consistently hitting capacity lilmits
, so I ultimately had to fall back to using on-demand instances. 

2. Describe a time when you embarked on a sidequest / took initiative outside your core job responsibilities. What value did you add to your organization? What drove you to action?
I designed a post-processing approoach for geospatial data that required standing up new database infrastructure. The team was wary of adding complexity to our tech stack, however I developed a
PoC showing the approach would bring significant improvements to our output quality that we weren't reaching otherwise. We ended up adopting the approach in our workflow.
